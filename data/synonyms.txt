#  Rules for edits:
#    * single words only (the C++ folding is per-token)
#    * label-safe swaps only: replacing a word with its alternate must never
#      change the row's labels (no urgency words in fillers, no cross-department
#      drift, no frustration-scale drift)
#    * conservative: a wrong merge here is a wrong lane in the fabric
# ============================================================================
